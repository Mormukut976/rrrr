Frequently asked questions

How long does recovery take?

Most cases are resolved within 24–48 hours. Complex disabled-account cases can take up to 3–5 working days.

What do you need from me?

Just the email linked to the account, your Instagram username, the reason, and a short description of the issue.

How do I pay?

We accept PayPal only. The flat fee is ₹1100 INR per account.

What if recovery fails?

If we cannot recover your account, your payment is refunded in full via PayPal.
